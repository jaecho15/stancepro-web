A visual prototype for the CAMP3 × StancePro product conversation. Eight screens, drawn to StancePro’s shipping design language and re-skinned in CAMP3’s orange and charcoal.

Every feature shown already exists in the app today. Nothing in the codebase was changed to make these — they are drawings, not builds. Tap any screen to see it full size.

Brand substitution

  • CAMP3 orange #F1682Dreplaces primaryNavy as the accent
  • CAMP3 charcoal #2B2B2Breplaces the navy ink
  • Cream ground #F9F7F0kept exactly as it ships
  • Dark-mode orange #FF7A45lifted for goggle legibility

StancePro’s dark theme accent is already an orange (#FF9900), chosen to stay legible through a goggle lens. CAMP3’s brand colour drops straight into that existing token — the light theme is the only real change.

Tab bar

The same five-slot shell StancePro ships, re-labelled Home · Schedule · Group · Ride · More. The surfaces underneath are Home, Events, the social hub, Ride Tracker and Settings. No navigation was rewritten.

What is not here

No coaching generated by a model, no prescriptions, no written feedback. The video screens show measured telemetry and nothing else. Camp booking and payment stay on camp3snowboarding.com; the app links out.

Status

An image mockup, 9 September 2026. No code, database, API or navigation structure was modified to produce it. Rider names, camp dates and figures are sample data.
